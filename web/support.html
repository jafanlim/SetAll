Frequently Asked Questions

Is SetAll free to use?

Yes, the core SetAll engine is completely free. We believe managing shared expenses shouldn't cost you extra money.

How does the "Greedy Flow" engine work?

Our engine algorithmically simplifies group debts. Instead of tracking dozens of complex IOUs, SetAll calculates the absolute minimum number of transfers required to settle the entire group, saving everyone time and bank fees.

Does SetAll connect to my bank account?

No. SetAll is a non-custodial ledger. We track the math, but we never touch your money. When it's time to settle up, you transfer the funds using your preferred method (Cash, Bank Transfer, PayPal) and mark it as settled in the app.

Can I use the app without an internet connection?

Yes. SetAll uses a local-first architecture. You can add expenses and create groups while entirely offline. The moment your device reconnects, it seamlessly syncs with the cloud in the background.

How do I delete my account?

Open the SetAll app, navigate to Settings → Delete Account, or go to your Account Portal → Danger Zone. Your data is permanently wiped from our servers within 30 days.
