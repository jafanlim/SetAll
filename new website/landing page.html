Greedy Flow Engine

Algorithmically minimises transactions. If Alice owes Bob and Bob owes Charlie, SetAll tells Alice to pay Charlie directly. Minimum transfers, zero waste.

Universal Anchor

150+ currencies including RSD, VND, and GEL. Spend in Japanese Yen, split in Euros, settle in USD. Every amount is anchored to its original rate at entry — no surprises.

Live Insights

Interactive financial canvas with Chart.js visualisations. Category donut, spending trend, group position — all pulled live from your Supabase ledger.

Local-First Architecture

Built on SQLite with conflict-free sync. Add expenses offline on a plane — the moment you reconnect, everything reconciles automatically in the background.

Group Balances

Shared groups with per-member splits, custom percentages, and direct 1-on-1 friend debts. Row-Level Security enforced at the database layer.

Non-Custodial & Secure

We never see your bank details. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Delete your account and every byte is wiped immediately — no ghost data.

How it works

Three steps to zero debt.

The Greedy Flow engine does the hard math so you don't have to.

01

Log any expense

Add an expense in any of 150+ currencies. SetAll anchors the amount to the live exchange rate at that exact moment, locking in accuracy forever.

02

Engine simplifies
