# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** March 13, 2026

## 1. Introduction

SetAll ("we", "our", or "us") operates the SetAll mobile and desktop application and the website at setall.app (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Account Information:** Email address and display name when you register.
- **Profile Data:** Optional avatar image and nickname.
- **Expense Data:** Expense names, amounts, currencies, and group membership data you enter.

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information
- **Usage Data:** App interactions, feature usage, and crash reports to improve the Service.
- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system version, and app version.
- **Push Notification Tokens:** FCM/APNs tokens to deliver expense notifications (only if you grant permission).

### 2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect
- We do not collect payment card details.
- We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
- We do not track your location.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Provide, operate, and maintain the Service.
- Sync your expense data across your devices in real time.
- Send you notifications about group activity (with your consent).
- Respond to support requests.
- Monitor and improve app performance.

## 4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Supabase (supabase.com), hosted on AWS infrastructure in the EU region. We implement industry-standard security measures including:
- TLS encryption in transit.
- Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensuring users can only access their own data.
- Bcrypt-hashed passwords (managed by Supabase Auth).

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share data with:
- **Supabase Inc.** — our backend-as-a-service provider (data processor).
- **Google Firebase** — for push notification delivery (FCM tokens only).
- **Law enforcement** — if required by applicable law.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your data is scheduled for permanent deletion within 30 days (the cooling-off window), after which it is irrecoverably purged.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:
- **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
- **Rectify** inaccurate data.
- **Erase** your data ("right to be forgotten") — use the Delete Account option in Settings.
- **Data portability** — contact us to request a data export.
- **Object** to processing — contact us at privacy@setall.app.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date above.
